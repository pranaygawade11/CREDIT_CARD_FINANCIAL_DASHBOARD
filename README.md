CREDIT_CARD_FINANCIAL_DASHBOARD_USING_POWERBI_&_SQL_&_EXCEL

# PROJECT OBJECTIVE
   An interactive Powerbi dashboard powered by SQL for analyzing data with KPIs and visual insights.

# PROBLEM STATEMENT
   To develop a comprehensive credit card weekly dashboard that provides real-time insights into key performance metrics and trends, enabling stakeholders to monitor and analyze credit card operations effectively.

# KPI REQUIREMNETS
   Total Revenue.
   Total Interest.
   Total Income.
   CSS.
   Revenue by Gender, Age group, QTR, Week, Card, top 5 States, Salary Group, Marital Status, Education Level.

# CHARTS AND VISUALISATION

[CREDIT CARD TRANSACTION REOPRT]
 1. QTR Revenue & Transaction Count = Sum of Revenue & Sum of Transaction Count by QTR using Bar Graph.
 2. Revenue by Expenditure = Sum of Revenue by Expenditure Type using Stacked Bar Graph.
 3.  Revenue by Education = Sum of Revenue by Type of Education using Stacked Bar Graph
 4.  Revenue by Customer Job = Sum of Revenue by Type of Customer Job using Stacked Bar Graph
 5.  Revenue by Customer Acquisition cost = Sum of Revenue by Type of card using Stacked Bar Graph
 6.  Revenue by Use Chip = Sum of Revenue by Type of Use Chip using Stacked Bar Graph

[CREDIT CARD CUSTOMER REPORT]
1. Revenue by Gender = Total Revenue by Gender using line Graph.
2. Top 5 States = Total Revenue by Top 5 States using stacked bar graph.
3. Salary Group = Total Revenue by Salary Group using stacked bar graph
4. Dependent Count = Total Revenue by dependents using stacked bar graph
5. Marital Status = Total Revenue by Marital Status using stacked bar graph
6. Education Level = Total Revenue by Education Level using stacked bar graph
7. Age Group = Total Revenue by Age Group using stacked bar graph
